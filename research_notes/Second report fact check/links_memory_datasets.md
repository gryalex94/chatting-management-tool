## Errors found

1. **No made-up papers, wrong IDs, wrong venues or wrong years** among these 12 links. Of particular note, arXiv 2609.03467 ("When Users Don't Ask", LOCOMO-CONV) is real, is dated September 2026, and contains every finding and limitation the report gives it.
2. **Minor wording, item 7 (MemGPT):** the report lists it among "Memory vendors publish…" papers. At publication it was an academic UC Berkeley paper; the company Letta came later. The advice itself, not to compare headline numbers across these papers, is still correct.
3. **Minor nuance, item 1 (LongMemEval):** "condensed facts can lose details" is correct as a general finding. However, the paper also found that splitting into facts *helped* multi-session reasoning, and the report leaves that out. This does not change the recommendation.
4. **Tiny wording, item 3 (LD-Agent):** the paper's module is called "persona extraction", not "persona management". The meaning is the same.
